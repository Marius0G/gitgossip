Git Gossip

The tech news developers are arguing about, explained in 60 seconds.

Every day Git Gossip picks the tech stories developers are actually talking about, like AI, open source, chips, security, startups and the occasional glorious disaster, and turns them into short videos. The voices are AI. Every story is approved by a human before it goes up.

  • Instagram
  • YouTube
  • TikTok

About the publishing tool

The videos are uploaded by a small private tool that the channel's owner runs on their own computer. It connects only to Git Gossip's own Instagram, YouTube and TikTok accounts, to upload videos and read how they perform. It is not offered to anyone else.
